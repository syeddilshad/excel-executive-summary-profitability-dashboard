# ABC Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard

## Project Overview

An interactive **Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard built entirely in Microsoft Excel** to analyse business performance across revenue, gross profit, sales volume, product categories, sales channels, regions and countries.

The dashboard transforms sales data into an executive-level reporting solution, enabling stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators, evaluate profitability trends, compare geographical performance and explore revenue distribution through interactive filters.

The project demonstrates practical Excel data analysis, business intelligence, data visualisation and dashboard development skills.

## Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Monitor overall revenue, gross profit and cost of goods sold (COGS).
- Evaluate gross profit margins and sales performance.
- Track monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Identify the top-performing countries by revenue.
- Analyse revenue contribution across different sales channels.
- Compare regional revenue distribution.
- Enable interactive business performance analysis using Excel slicers.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes six executive-level KPIs:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Overall revenue generated |
| Total Gross Profit | Revenue remaining after COGS |
| Gross Profit Margin | Gross profit as a percentage of revenue |
| Total Units Sold | Total quantity of products sold |
| Total Orders | Total number of orders |
| Total COGS | Total cost of goods sold |

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive KPI Overview
A consolidated view of the key financial and operational performance indicators, providing a quick understanding of business performance.

### 2. Monthly Revenue & Profit Analysis
Tracks monthly revenue and gross profit trends to understand changes in business performance throughout the year.

### 3. Top 5 Countries by Revenue
Compares revenue generated across the leading countries to identify important geographical markets.

### 4. Revenue Share by Sales Channel
Evaluates the contribution of different sales channels:
- Corporate
- Marketplace
- Online Store
- Retail Partners

### 5. Region-Wise Comparison
Compares revenue distribution across geographical regions to understand regional business performance.

### 6. Country-Wise Revenue Map
Provides a geographical representation of revenue distribution across countries, helping identify market concentration.

## Interactive Dashboard Capabilities

The dashboard incorporates Excel slicers for dynamic filtering and exploration.

Available filters:
- Product Category
- Country
- Quarter

Users can interact with these filters to explore business performance across different categories, geographical markets and reporting periods.
